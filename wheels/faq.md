# Frequently asked questions

The client edits this file. Each question is a line that starts with "## ", and the lines under
it are the answer.

## Which documents do I need?
Your driving licence, your CPR card or passport, and a bank card in the name of the driver.

## Can I return the car on the day I pick it up?
Yes. A same-day return is charged as one day.

## Is fuel included?
No. You get the car with a full tank and bring it back full.

## Can I drive to Saudi Arabia?
Only with our Causeway pack, which adds the insurance Saudi Arabia asks for. Ask at the desk.

## How do I change my booking?
Call the desk or write to us with your booking code, like WH-1001.
